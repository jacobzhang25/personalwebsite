This is the first post on this site. It exists mainly to prove that the
plumbing works — Markdown comes in, a page goes out — but it's also a
convenient place to say what I want this thing to be.

## Why another personal site

I wanted a place to think out loud that I fully control. Not Substack,
not Medium, not a tweet thread. A directory of Markdown files, rendered
with care, that I own.

## What to expect

I'm going to write about a few different things, and I want the site
itself to reflect that. Each post is tagged with a **sphere** — a rough
category for the area of life it belongs to:

- **work** — notes on what I'm building
- **spirituality** — the long questions
- **creativity** — making things for no reason
- **technology** — tools, systems, how they bend us
- **personal** — everything else

Eventually the blog index will become a branching visualization where
each sphere is a branch and posts hang off the relevant one. For now,
it's just a list.

## Writing style

Short paragraphs. Real sentences. No SEO. If a post turns into a book,
that's fine; if it stays two paragraphs, that's fine too.

> The best way to write is to put words down and then cross most of
> them out.

Thanks for reading. More soon.
